```

**Based on:**
- Number of agents (per million: +$5k)
- Number of seats (per user: +$500/year)

```

### Sales Playbook

**Enterprise Sales Process:**

1. **Discovery (Week 1-2)**
   - Pain points: simulation cost, time-to-market, realism
   - Current tools: Gazebo, CARLA, custom
   - Team size & budget

2. **Technical Evaluation (Week 3-6)**
   - POC: Run customer scenario
   - Performance benchmarks
   - Integration requirements

3. **Pilot (Week 7-14)**
   - 8-week pilot with 1-2 scenarios
   - Success criteria: 50% time reduction, 30% cost savings
   - Pilot cost: $10k (applied to contract)

4. **Enterprise Contract (Week 15+)**
   - 3-year deal
   - Annual: $200k-$500k
   - Includes: consulting, training, support

**Sales Targets:**
- Year 1: 5 enterprise customers ($1M ARR)
- Year 2: 20 enterprise customers ($5M ARR)
- Year 3: 50 enterprise customers ($15M ARR)
